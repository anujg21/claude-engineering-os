## Problem

Who has this problem, how do they handle it today, and what does that cost them?
Three sentences. No solution here.

## Primary user

One role or one named person. What they are trying to get done, and what stops them now.
List secondary users separately and say how their needs differ.

## Why now

What changed that makes this worth doing this quarter rather than next year.

## Success

The measurable change that means this worked. A number, a baseline, and a date.

## Scope

**In scope for the first release.** The smallest thing that delivers the value above.

**Explicitly out of scope.** The things people will assume are included. Naming them here
is what makes this section useful later.

## Constraints

Deadline, budget, team, systems it must work with, compliance and data obligations.

## Risks and assumptions

The riskiest assumption first: the one that, if wrong, means this should not be built.
State how it could be tested cheaply.

- `ASSUMPTION:` treated as true without confirmation, and what breaks if it is wrong.
- `OPEN:` unanswered, and what it blocks.

## Alternatives considered

What else would solve this problem, including doing nothing and buying something.
Why this one.
